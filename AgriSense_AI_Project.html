Project Overview

AgriSense AI is an AI-powered precision agriculture monitoring system that integrates multispectral satellite imagery and IoT environmental sensor data to provide real-time crop health insights.

The system detects vegetation stress, disease outbreaks, and pest risks by combining spectral analysis with temporal AI models, enabling farmers to shift from reactive to proactive precision agriculture.

This project addresses Problem Statement 13 from the 4th Semester AI/ML curriculum.

AI Models
🧠 ResNet50 CNN
38-class crop disease classifier. Fine-tuned on PlantVillage dataset with ~95% validation accuracy.
📈 LSTM + Attention
3-layer LSTM with Bahdanau attention. 30-day look-back → 7-day pest risk forecast.
🔀 U-Net Segmentation
Field zone segmentation from satellite imagery. Identifies crop types and health zones.
